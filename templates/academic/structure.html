Academic Structure

Manage your school's annual Academic Sessions and their sequential operating Terms.

Distinction: Academic Sessions vs. Academic Terms

1. Academic Session: The overarching full school year (e.g. 2024/2025). Defines the calendar year boundaries for the school.
2. Academic Term: The operating periods within each session (e.g. First Term, Second Term, Third Term). Student enrollments, fee structures, and billing are recorded against a specific Term.
